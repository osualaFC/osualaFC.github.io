Built to help you remember

Nchéta is an Igbo word meaning "to remember." We built this app on a simple belief: the gap between reading something and remembering it shouldn't require willpower alone. AI should help you learn, not just answer questions.

n · CHEH · tah

Everything you need to learn deeply

Not just another flashcard app. A complete learning system powered by AI and cognitive science.

∞

AI Generation

Paste text, upload PDFs, scan textbook pages with your camera, or dictate voice notes. AI instantly creates flashcards, MCQs, and summaries.

⟳

Spaced Repetition

FSRS algorithm schedules reviews at the perfect moment — right before you forget. Your daily dashboard shows exactly what needs attention.

◈

Mixed Reviews

Flashcards and quiz questions interleave in review sessions. See source explanations when you get something wrong. Rate difficulty to train the algorithm.

↗

Adaptive Difficulty

Master a concept at the "remember" level? Nchéta automatically generates harder "apply" and "analyze" questions using Bloom's taxonomy.

▦

Study Analytics

GitHub-style heatmaps, weekly stats, concept mastery breakdown, and a 7-day review forecast. See exactly how your knowledge is growing.

◎

Focus Mode

Monochrome design eliminates visual distraction. Focus mode silences notifications during study sessions so you can concentrate fully.

From content to mastery in four steps

01
Capture

Add any content — paste text, upload a PDF, photograph a page with your camera, or record your voice. Nchéta accepts it all.

02
Generate

AI analyzes your material and creates a set of flashcards, multiple-choice questions, and a concise summary in seconds.

03
Review

Work through your daily queue. Rate each card. The FSRS algorithm schedules the next review at the exact moment you need it.

04
Master

Watch your heatmap fill in and mastery scores climb. Nchéta escalates difficulty as you improve, ensuring lasting retention.

Challenge anyone with Showdowns

Create a showdown, share a code, and anyone can join instantly. Real-time scores, question-level analysis, and complete privacy — free for everyone, no premium required.

  • Build showdowns with AI-generated or manual questions
  • Share via 6-character code or QR — scan to join
  • Timed showdowns with no answer peeking
  • Per-player scores and question-level analysis
  • Completely free for everyone — no premium needed
