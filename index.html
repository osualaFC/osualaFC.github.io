Built to help you remember

Nchéta is an Igbo word meaning "to remember." We built this app on a simple belief: the gap between reading something and remembering it shouldn't require willpower alone. AI should help you learn, not just answer questions.

Everything you need to learn deeply

Not just another flashcard app — a complete learning system powered by AI and cognitive science.

AI generation

Paste text, upload PDFs, scan a textbook page, or dictate a voice note. AI instantly creates flashcards, MCQs and summaries.

Spaced repetition

The FSRS algorithm schedules reviews at the perfect moment — right before you forget. Your dashboard shows what needs attention.

Mixed reviews

Flashcards and quiz questions interleave in each session. See source explanations when you get something wrong, and rate difficulty.

Adaptive difficulty

Master a concept at the "remember" level and Nchéta generates harder "apply" and "analyze" questions using Bloom's taxonomy.

Study analytics

GitHub-style heatmaps, weekly stats, concept mastery breakdown and a 7-day review forecast. See your knowledge grow.

Focus mode

A calm, distraction-free design. Focus mode silences notifications during study sessions so you can concentrate fully.

From content to mastery in four steps
